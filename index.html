About Me

HIPAA-Certified Medical Virtual Assistant & Registered Nurse

I'm a HIPAA-certified Medical Virtual Assistant and Registered Nurse with a rare blend of clinical insight and operational precision. With 2+ years as a dedicated Medical Virtual Assistant and 5+ years of clinical nursing experience, I help clinics, hospices, and private practices streamline their daily operations.

Advanced EMR/EHR fluency across platforms like AthenaHealth, Kareo, DrChrono, and TheraNest allows me to transform fragmented workflows into streamlined operations—reducing scheduling conflicts, accelerating insurance verification, and driving measurable improvements in patient satisfaction scores.

What if your administrative systems worked as hard as you do? I turn that "what if" into a daily routine. My approach isn't reactive; it's architectural. I design scalable systems for telehealth coordination, medical documentation, patient intake, and revenue cycle support—so your practice runs seamlessly.

The result? You gain back 40 hours a week, reduce administrative burnout, and reclaim the mental bandwidth to lead, not just manage. Because when your systems are optimized, your patients feel it—and so do you.
